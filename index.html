      Questions

      Before you commit.

      Can’t find what you need? Press ? for keyboard shortcuts, or ⌘K to jump anywhere on this page.

      Do I have to replace my current tracing setup?

      No. Orrery accepts OpenTelemetry, Zipkin and Jaeger formats directly. Most teams point their existing exporters at Orrery and keep their current dashboards running while they compare.

      How is a span counted?

      One span is one timed operation, such as an HTTP request or a database query. Logs and attributes attached to a span don’t count separately.

      Where is my data stored?

      In the region you choose when you create a workspace: the US, the EU or APAC. Data stays in that region, backups included.

      What happens if I go over my plan?

      Ingestion keeps running. On Observatory you pay the per-million rate for the extra spans. On Spyglass, Orrery samples down to your limit and warns you a few days before it starts.

      Can I self-host Orrery?

      Array customers can run the full platform in their own cloud account on Kubernetes. It ships as a signed Helm chart on the same release schedule as the hosted product.

      How long does setup take?

      Most teams see their first traces within 15 minutes of running npx orrery init. Drift baselines are ready after about an hour of traffic.
